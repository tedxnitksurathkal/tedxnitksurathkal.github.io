# Asset provenance

## Decorative hero

`assets/img/quiet-signal/threshold-1600.webp` and `threshold-800.webp` are responsive exports of original artwork generated with the built-in imagegen tool for this implementation. The larger export is about 94 KB and the smaller about 32 KB. The PNG design-kit references were inspected for composition but are not embedded as UI or cropped into text-bearing backgrounds.

Generation prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: cinematic atmospheric website hero background, landscape 1536x1024 or wider. Create original concept artwork for 'The Quiet Signal', noise dissolving into clarity. No words, no letters, no numbers, no logo, no UI, no borders. Nearly black charcoal cinematic environment; left 45 percent is dark empty smoky negative space for website typography. On the right half, a tall jagged wall of obsidian shards, delicate tangled silver filament wires and tiny muted red sparks dissolves toward a narrow vertical warm white-red light fissure at x=75 percent. Beyond it a quiet distant mountain horizon and soft dawn mist, a very small lone human silhouette at the threshold to establish monumental scale. Rocky black ground, subtle warm reflected light, natural detailed material, refined dramatic film photography, realistic volumetric atmosphere, restrained red #E62B1E accent. Strong black shadows, no neon, no saturated orange sky, no sci-fi machinery. The light and silhouette stay right of center; leave left half readable dark negative space. This is metaphorical landscape, not a real venue.

## Existing photography and branding

- `assets/img/quiet-signal/community.webp` is a resized/compressed export of the existing `assets/img/bg/team.jpg` photograph. Original retained.
- Speaker portraits are existing files in `assets/img/past_editions/`, with identities and historical roles from `assets/js/past-editions.js`.
- Team portraits and roles come from the original `assets/js/team-list.js`.
- TEDxNITKSurathkal and Salon wordmarks retain their existing image assets and intrinsic proportions. No logo was redrawn.
- Previous sponsor images are existing Domino’s and McDonald’s assets. They are not presented as confirmed partners of the new theme.
- Archive thumbnails use the original YouTube video IDs. The corresponding talks and direct watch links are preserved.

## Fonts and graphics

- Bodoni Moda: self-hosted Latin WOFF2 from Google Fonts. Source: `https://fonts.gstatic.com/s/bodonimoda/v28/aFTH7PxzY382XsXX63LUYL6GYFcan6NJrKp-VPj1KOxQ.woff2`. SIL Open Font License is included at `assets/fonts/Bodoni-Moda-OFL.txt`.
- Gilroy Regular and Bold: pre-existing font files in `assets/theme/fonts/`; reused without changes. Their original licensing should remain part of the repository’s asset review.
- Signal line illustrations are original lightweight SVG paths generated by the shared template, with no external library or animation loop.
